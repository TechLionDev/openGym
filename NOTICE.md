# Provenance Notice — openGym (TechLionDev private recovery)

This repository is a **private recovery copy** of **openGym**, the self-hosted
gym & body-weight tracker originally published by DuarteSantos8 at
`github.com/DuarteSantos8/openGym`.

## Why this exists
- The original repository and its author account were deleted in early August 2026
  (last public snapshot archived 2026-08-01 via web.archive.org).
- The source code was recovered from a public fork:
  `github.com/arvids-unavailable/openGym` (faithful AGPL-3.0 fork, ~364 stars).
- Preserved privately by **TechLionDev** on 2026-08-19 for internal reference/study.
